Changes: three complete 16 kHz utterances were concatenated with two 0.5-second
silent gaps. A shared gain was applied to both output clips. The noisy version
adds synthetic Gaussian white noise at 10 dB whole-recording RMS SNR (including
the inserted gaps), using NumPy default_rng with seed 42. The noise is generated
for this recipe; it is NOT a Microsoft environmental-noise recording.
